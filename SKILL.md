# XXD Panel 012 · 生命色几何线场

Turn each supplied photograph into a finished geometric line-field poster. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must retain the same source's identity, silhouette, pose, and narrative relation while making it emerge from a dense living field of repeated lines. Offset contours, short returns, and slight jitter create unstable internal motion; thinner and sparser homologous lines diffuse outward while rational axes, arcs, tangents, and implicit grids hold the field in exact editorial order. One clean, vivid main line colour is chosen for the source's spirit rather than its area.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve source identity through at least three specific cues while rebuilding the subject with a source-earned density field: dense repeated lines, offset contours, short returns, and slight jitter aggregate into a recognisable centre; thinner, lighter, sparser homologous lines diffuse outward; rational axes and geometric constraints stabilise the free motion. It is not literal tracing, random scribbling, a directionless tangle, generic wireframe, cyber-tech HUD, cartoon, glossy vector, photoreal painting, 3D render, or unrelated flourish.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 012 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relationship → preserve at least three identity, silhouette, pose, action, structure, or narrative-relation cues → select the colour that best carries the subject's vitality and spirit, not simply the largest colour area → aggregate the subject from high-density repeated lines, offset contours, short returns, and slight jitter while keeping a crisp recognisable edge and unstable flowing interior → diffuse thinner, lighter, sparser homologous lines outward → impose horizontal, vertical, diagonal, circular, tangent, or implicit-grid order earned by the source → hold centre-high/periphery-low density, one focus, generous white space, and tension between rational geometry and free line motion → integrate one short title plus a few precise black-grey editorial micro-elements along axes, contours, and field boundaries**.

The line behaviour comes from the source's actual silhouette, pose, action, direction, relation, internal rhythm, and visible energy—not from adding stock waves, arbitrary scribbles, decorative diagrams, or generic data graphics. Experimental printmaking, kinetic drawing, modernist grids, independent publishing, and precision editorial design are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the recognisable edge, density centre, line direction, geometric scaffold, main colour, or copy. Also reject literal outline drawing, random doodling, directionless knots, uniformly dense wireframes, fixed blue palettes, muddy desaturation, faux vintage, multicolour clutter, digital gradients, cyber-tech styling, glossy vector polish, photoreal shading, 3D volume, commercial advertising, cartoons, or template-like symmetry.

## 012 visual system

### Source identity and density-built subject

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across silhouette, pose/orientation, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, relationship distance, action, or dominant direction.
- Identify the subject's strongest visible energy: tension, acceleration, stillness under pressure, growth, balance, collision, gathering, dispersal, or another source-supported state. Convert it into line density, offset, return, jitter, and directional flow rather than stock symbolism.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep source-specific terrain, horizon, or spatial relation.
- Preserve a clear recognition edge while letting the interior remain unstable, layered, and alive. The subject must emerge from line accumulation rather than a single finished outline or explanatory diagram.

### Centre-high, periphery-low line field

- Use a clean white, warm white, or very pale source-compatible ground with generous open space, one unmistakable focus, and no atmospheric dirt.
- Build the subject centre from high-density repeated lines, offset contour passes, short return loops, and controlled slight jitter. Density, thickness, overlap, and compression establish depth without shading or volume effects.
- Continue the same line family beyond the subject with thinner, lighter, and sparser traces. The falloff must be legible: highest density where identity and energy concentrate, lowest density toward the perimeter.
- Every outward trace needs a directional relationship to the source. Do not surround the subject with an evenly distributed halo, random spaghetti, filler rays, or decorative turbulence.

### Free motion held by geometric order

- Introduce only the horizontal, vertical, diagonal, circular, arc, tangent, or implicit-grid structures that clarify the source's direction, balance, relation, or pace.
- Let those axes restrain, cut through, align, measure, or counterweight the freely moving line field. Geometry is quiet structural pressure, not a visible software grid or scientific interface.
- Keep line character precise yet bodily: slight variation, offset, friction, and return are welcome; careless scribble, smooth Bézier sterility, uniform wireframe, and chaotic knots are not.
- Preserve flat print depth. Reject realistic modelling, digital gradients, cinematic glow, neon, metal 3D, clay, resin, plastic, HUD overlays, and shadow effects.

### One source-derived vitality colour

- Select one main line colour from the source for vitality, recognition, and the subject's spirit—not by averaging pixels or choosing the largest colour patch. It may be modestly purified, brightened, or corrected so it reads clean, vivid, and contemporary.
- Use that one colour consistently across the active line field, with black and grey reserved for microtype and minor structural marks. Tonal variation may come from line density, thickness, and overlap, not from gradients.
- Reject a fixed blue look, muddy greys, artificially aged colour, multiple competing hues, rainbow palettes, broad colour fills, and digital gradients. The chosen main colour must remain visibly traceable to the source.

## Copy and typography belong to 012

012 text behaves like a precise editorial measuring system that steadies the restless line field—not a detached headline, slogan wall, scientific HUD, or generic magazine block.

- Automatic copy derives one extremely short title from the source's visible or supported emotion, action, time, state, relation, or metaphor, plus only the useful combination of a state word, restrained philosophical line, number, scale mark, or micro-note.
- Factual-looking dates, places, provenance, measurements, coordinates, and numbers must be supplied or reliably established. Abstract index numbers may be visibly coded as compositional markers rather than facts.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 012 construction:

- **Role:** a rational editorial counterforce that names the source-specific state and makes the line field feel measured rather than chaotic.
- **Voice:** concise, observant, sharp, restrained, alive, and conceptually precise.
- **Construction:** one short title plus a few much smaller supporting elements; use thin weight, wide spacing, native small-cap equivalent, vertical setting, rotation, indexing, or asymmetry only when natural to the script.
- **Placement:** align to a geometric axis, subject contour, density boundary, tangent, or implicit grid; allow type to rotate, cross, interrupt, or offset against the line field while remaining legible.
- **Material:** clean black-grey editorial print with subtle paper interaction, never glowing UI text or a detachable digital overlay.
- **Script:** use a native precision-editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction rather than forcing Latin spacing or rotation onto every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

## Fresh-task and source boundary

Every invocation starts a fresh generation job unless the user explicitly asks to continue, audit, compare, edit, or reuse a named earlier result. Repeating the same source and parameters still means generating a new result in a new task directory. An old file can never satisfy a new request.

## Composition commands

```bash

  --out result.png --layout top-bottom

python3 scripts/compose_panel.py --source photo.png --design design.png \
  --out result.png --layout left-right

  --out result.png --layout design-only
```
